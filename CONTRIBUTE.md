# Contributing to this project

1. Follow the best practices followed on .skills folder.

## Your books on your public profile

Add `public/books/<your-github-login-in-lowercase>.md` in a pull request, with a table of your books (a link with the title, the total pages and the cover address). The format and the rules are in the FAQ: https://university.kurz.fyi/faq#books. A check on the pull request confirms that its author owns the file.
