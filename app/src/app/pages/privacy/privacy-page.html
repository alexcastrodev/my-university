Privacy notice: the Kurz

Version {{ version }}

What the Kurz is

The Kurz is an assistant that answers questions about the post you are reading. It answers only from that post and from the posts it links to, and it shows where each answer comes from. It can still be wrong, so check the citations.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. The first time you ask, you confirm that and that you read this notice. Asking costs credits, which an admin grants. Without credits you can still use "Open in" to take a passage to another assistant.

What is sent to Google

To write an answer we send Google (the Gemini API) your question, the text you selected, the post you are on and a short summary of the posts it links to, and the last two exchanges of that conversation. We do not send your name, your email, your notes, your papers or anything from other people. We turn off the storage of the conversation on Google's side, and we use the paid plan, under which Google does not use it to improve its products.

"Open in" another assistant

"Open in" sends the passage and the question you wrote, with the link of the post, to the service you pick (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini), in the link that opens it or through your clipboard. That happens outside University: we keep nothing of it, and the privacy terms of that service apply.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Your conversations (questions and answers) are kept for 90 days after the last message, and then erased.
  • The count of what each question used (tokens, credits, date) is kept, with no text, to keep the bill right.
  • If you report a wrong answer, we keep a copy of that exchange for the team to review, without your name, even after you erase your conversations.
  • What you publish in a discussion is an ordinary comment: erasing your conversations does not erase it.

Who sees it

Your conversations are private: only you see them, not other students and not the admins. The admins see how many credits each person used, never the words. Errors that reach us are cleaned of what you wrote.

Your control

You can erase one conversation, or all of them, whenever you want, in&ngsp;Settings.
